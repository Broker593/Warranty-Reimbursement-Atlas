A 50-state quick reference to dealer warranty-reimbursement law: labor rates, paid hours, parts, claims and chargebacks, and whether service contracts and CPO are covered. Built from public statutes.

Glossary
Hourly rate vs. paid hours
Labor payment = hourly rate × allowed hours. A time multiplier changes the hours; Wisconsin builds the time difference into the rate.
Posted vs. effective labor rate
Posted is the advertised rate. Effective is labor revenue divided by the hours in the qualifying sample.
Parts markup vs. gross margin
A $100 part reimbursed at $160 is a 60% markup and a 37.5% gross margin.
Sample period vs. age limit
The sample period is how much repair-order history to submit; the age limit is how old it can be.
